How a Project Runs

Commercial projects generally move through four phases. Here is what happens in each.

  1. 01

    Preconstruction

    Review the drawings, price the work, and set the schedule, so scope, budget, and timeline are agreed before work starts.

  2. 02

    Site & Structure

    Site work, foundations, and the structure where the job calls for them, with the trades scheduled and coordinated by the general contractor.

  3. 03

    Build-Out & Systems

    The envelope, interiors, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing work, sequenced so trades are not waiting on each other.

  4. 04

    Closeout & Handoff

    Punch list, final inspections, and closeout documents, so the space is finished and the paperwork is in hand when you move in.
