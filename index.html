How a Job Runs

Most jobs move through four steps. Here is what happens in each.

  1. 01

    Scope & Pricing

    Review the plans or scope of work and price it, so the budget is agreed before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Scheduling

    Set dates and sequence the work with your team, so everyone knows when crews will be on site.

  3. 03

    Build & Install

    Build-out, fixtures, displays, casework, electrical, and signage, carried out to the plans.

  4. 04

    Closeout

    Punch list, final walkthrough, and closeout paperwork, so the space is finished and ready to use.
